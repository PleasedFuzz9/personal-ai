Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Overview

Personal AI is a personal-use application designed to collect and analyze health and wellness data from authorized sources, including the Oura API.

2. Information Collected

With authorization, the application may access sleep, activity, readiness, heart rate, heart rate variability, workouts, stress, blood oxygen, and other permitted Oura data.

3. How Data Is Used

Data is used for personal analytics, identifying trends, tracking wellness, and generating AI-assisted insights.

4. Storage and Security

The application is intended to store personal data in a privately controlled database. Reasonable precautions will be taken to restrict unauthorized access and protect authentication credentials.

5. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party AI services to process selected data. When enabled, data submitted to those services is subject to their applicable privacy policies and processing terms. The application does not sell personal health information.

6. Data Sharing

Personal information is not publicly published or sold. Data is shared only with services necessary for authorized application functionality.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is intended to be retained only as needed for personal analytics. Stored data may be deleted from the application database. Access to Oura data can be revoked through Oura's account settings.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when application functionality or data practices change.

9. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application developer using the email listed in the Oura developer registration.
